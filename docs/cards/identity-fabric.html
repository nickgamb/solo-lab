Pre-demo setup

About 30 minutes the first time; image caches outlive the cluster.

#Run thisWhat it does
1 Once per machine
make machine-setup
 One sudo: *.lab resolves locally and the lab CA is trusted.
2 Build the lab
make up && make verify
 Every layer and demo. Must end with all checks passed.
3 Left screen Open https://observatory.ops.lab as ops, tab Identity Continuity The fabric, live.
Running it again

Put the rule back in Routing policy (Reset, or undo the edit and Save), and Restore IdP network.

The run-through

#Do thisSay this
1 Observatory Point at the map, left to right The fabric Resources and MCP servers on the left, the gateway in the middle, every IdP the firm owns on the right. Nothing on the left knows which IdP signed anyone in.
2 Observatory Click Routing policy Routing is policy Which IdP a sign-in goes to is a rule at the gateway, in CEL. First match wins; each rule lists IdPs in order.
3 Observatory Tab Gateway policy agentgateway runs it Written from the rules and every IdP's health, rewritten on every failover. No client can pick its own IdP.
4 Terminal
make routes
 One door, the right IdP A browser app goes to the primary; an AI client goes to the IdP the firm runs itself, with MFA.
5 Observatory Tab Rules: change ai-clients -> keycloak to ai-clients -> contingency, keycloak, then Save Live One edit, no app or client changes.
6 Terminal
make routes
 Moved AI clients now sign in at the contingency IdP.
7 Observatory Pick Sterling & Vance (contingency), click Simulate IdP outage, then make routes Its IdP is down The rule falls back down its own list, to S&V's Keycloak, on its own.
8 Right screen
What is Ledgerline's view on technology, and which Ledgerline account am I using?
 One issuer A SaaS that trusts one issuer answers as Bob's own account, whichever IdP he used.
9 Observatory Click Restore IdP network, and put the rule back Identity fabric, by Solo Every IdP to every resource and MCP server: one door for people and agents, the policy at the gateway.
