Gluu Demo Card

Gluu is the firm's IdP and Ledgerline's authorization server. Bob's agent reaches Ledgerline as Bob through Cross App Access.

Left screen Terminal in the solo-lab checkout
Right screen kagent https://kagent.sterling.lab

Pre-demo setup

Needs two Gluu servers, one for each company. Registration: docs/GLUU.md.

#Run thisWhat it does
1 Once per machine
make machine-setup
 One sudo: *.lab resolves locally and the lab CA is trusted.
2 Settings
cp .env.example .env
 Set ENTERPRISE_IDP=gluu,keycloak, GLUU_ISSUER, GLUU_CLIENT_ID, RESOURCE_AS=gluu, RESOURCE_AS_ISSUER.
3 Build the lab
make up
 Every layer and demo, with Gluu in both roles.
4 Right screen, once Sign in to kagent as bob at Gluu, then to https://idp.ledgerline.lab/realms/ledgerline/account through Gluu Links Bob's Gluu account at S&V and at Ledgerline.
5 Check
make bob-verify
 Must end with all checks passed.
