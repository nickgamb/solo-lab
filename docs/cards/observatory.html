Pre-demo setup

The lab is up (make up) with a model behind it (make llm): Bob's questions go to his real agent. The outage scene also needs the upstream IdP configured (docs/IDENTITY-CONTINUITY.md); without it, the tour skips that scene.

Pacing

The tour waits for Enter between scenes, so you talk at your own speed. make tour TOUR_AUTO=1 runs it hands-free (TOUR_PAUSE seconds between scenes). TOUR_FROM=4 starts at a scene.

Running it again

make reset, then the tour. Each scene prints where to look, so the terminal doubles as your script.

The run-through

The terminal on the left drives, and the Observatory on the right shows it. Press Enter after each scene.

#Do thisSay this
1 Terminal
make tour
 Real traffic Nothing here is mocked. The tour signs in as Bob, asks his actual agent questions, and runs refusals from pods with their own mesh identities. The Observatory only watches.
2 Scene 1 · Topology Watch the bob-assistant tray, then the wires out of it Delegation A worker bay lights as Bob's agent wakes in its sandbox. Its calls go to the model through ai-gateway, and to Bob's workspace through the MCP waypoint (the badge on the wire). The agent answers acting for bob.
3 Traffic Filter mcp, turn on Carries a token, expand tools/call whoami Two identities Bob's verified claims, on the exact call. The waypoint checked Bob's token (audience, advisor group, expiry) and the agent's workload identity, then handed the tool a different, two-minute token. The raw token never reaches this screen, only its claims.
4 Scene 2 · Topology Hover ai-gateway Cross App Access Out to another company, and back with an answer. ai-gateway reaches Ledgerline Research through the edge. It traded Bob's ID token for an ID-JAG, and Ledgerline issued its own token. The agent held neither of them.
5 Scene 3 · Traffic Outcome filter: denied only Refused Seven bypasses, and none of them got through. They were refused by the tool filter, the waypoint's policy (no user token; Bob's token from another namespace; Bob's token from a pod beside the agents that isn't one), the mesh (a pod dialed directly), the egress gateway, and Ledgerline itself. The terminal prints which layer refused each one.
6 Topology View Cross-party Boundaries Only the calls between companies are left. Every one of them goes through the front door, the way separate companies meet on the internet.
7 Scene 4 · Topology Watch Meridian's and Alice's lanes Her terms Bob's agent asks for Alice's portfolio, which Meridian holds. Meridian's gateway sends it to Alice's authorization server. She has never met this agent, so it waits until she approves (the tour approves as her), and the holdings come back. Her portal's Activity ledger records all of it.
8 Scene 5 · Identity Continuity Switch tabs before pressing Enter Outage A real network cut to the upstream IdP, at the firm's egress. Amber while the health checks fail, then red: FAILOVER ACTIVE, about ten seconds later. New sign-ins now get the firm's own login, and every app keeps trusting the same issuer.
9 Identity Continuity Press Enter once more Failback Network restored, and after three healthy checks it's green again. Transitions at the bottom records the cut, the failover, the restore and the failback.
10 Topology Export (bottom left, under the zoom controls) Take it with you The whole map as a PNG, in whichever view you're on. It's drawn from the running cluster, so another lab renders the same way.
