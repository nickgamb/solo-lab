Identity Continuity Demo Card

Take the firm's IdP off the network. Sign-in moves to the firm's own accounts, and nothing downstream notices.

Left screen Observatory, Identity Continuity tab https://observatory.ops.lab
Right screen A private browser window, for Bob's sign-ins https://kagent.sterling.lab

Pre-demo setup

Needs an Auth0 tenant (free tier): docs/IDENTITY-CONTINUITY.md, Auth0 setup.

#Run thisWhat it does
1 Once per machine
make machine-setup
 One sudo: *.lab resolves locally and the lab CA is trusted.
2 Settings
cp .env.example .env
 Set AUTH0_ISSUER, AUTH0_CLIENT_ID, AUTH0_CLIENT_SECRET.
3 Build the lab
make up && make verify
 Every layer and demo. Must end with all checks passed.
4 Check
make status
 Sign-in: active: auth0.
5 Left screen https://observatory.ops.lab as ops / ops-demo, then Identity Continuity Green banner: CONNECTED.
Running it again

Restore IdP network ends the outage.

Unattended

make continuity-verify

The run-through

#Do thisSay this
1 Observatory Point at the map, left to right One issuer Every app trusts one issuer: the firm's Keycloak.
2 Right screen Open kagent in a private window. On Auth0's page, sign in as bob@sterling.lab Brokered Auth0's login page, then kagent, as Bob.
3 Observatory Click Simulate IdP outage Network cut This isn't a flag: it's a real network cut.
4 Observatory Watch the banner for about ten seconds Failover Amber, counting failed checks. Then red: FAILOVER ACTIVE.
5 Right screen Open a new private window, open kagent, and sign in as bob / bob-demo Still Bob The firm's own login form, and the same Bob.
6 Terminal
kubectl --context kind-solo-lab -n sv-egress get authorizationpolicy -L continuity.lab.solo.io/tier
 Auditable The whole outage is one policy: continuity-partition-auth0, DENY.
7 Observatory Click Restore IdP network Failback Amber, counting healthy checks, then green again.
8 Observatory Point at Transitions and the rule builder on the right Any IdP The sequence was cut, failover, restore, failback, and every step is recorded.
